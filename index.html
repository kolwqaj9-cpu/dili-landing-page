Dilisights is the operating name of Strata Data LLC, a New Mexico company.

We bring the customer. You do the work.

Strata Data LLC is a B2B digital marketing company. We run reference properties that industrial buyers already use, and we deliver the client inquiries that come out of them to third-party logistics providers, freight forwarders and commercial electricians. We are paid by those providers for the inquiries we deliver.

What we do Talk to us

  • Who we serve

    Third-party logistics (3PL) providers, freight forwarders and commercial electrical contractors serving industrial and commercial clients in the United States. What we do for them.

  • Where the inquiries come from

    Our own reference properties: page-cited lookups built from manuals and parts catalogs a maker already publishes, and reports built from public federal records. Industrial buyers arrive looking for an answer, and some of them need someone to do the work. The properties.

  • How we get paid

    By the service provider, for inquiries delivered. No monthly retainer is required to start, we do not take a cut of your invoice, and we do not sell the same inquiry twice. Pricing.
