Dilisights is the operating name of Strata Data LLC, Albuquerque, New Mexico.

We turn public documents into pages a working technician can use.

Two lines of work. A maker's own manual or parts catalog becomes a one-question lookup with page cites. Public federal records become a report with charts, tables and CSV. We are not the equipment maker, and we do not sell search ranking.

What we make How to pay

  • Manuals and catalogs → cited lookups

    One question that is already answered somewhere in a book the maker publishes. The page shows the steps and the page number. If a cite is wrong, say so — that is the test.

  • Public records → published reports

    We read federal injury, inspection, award and filing records, clean them, count them, and publish the charts, the tables and the CSV. More on the data desk.

  • How we get paid

    Fifteen US dollars per finished page, invoiced after delivery. Ten pages from the same public book are one hundred fifty dollars. Reports we publish from public records are free to republish with attribution. No monthly rank contract.

Received a note or a private link from us?

If someone mailed you a short clip and a private link, this is the shop that sent it. Open the maker's own PDF and check the cites before anyone works on a machine. Start here.
