A spreadsheet row, turned into a product page

Below are twenty pages. Each one started as a single row — brand, model, input rating, efficiency — taken from the public ENERGY STAR certified-product table. The rows are real; the writing is the sample. Nothing here is our catalog.

What this shows: how a parts spreadsheet reads once it is written out as pages on a site. What it does not show: your SKUs, your lead times, your freight notes — those stay yours and go on your domain.

If you want a trial: send fifty rows from your own spreadsheet (SKU, name, the columns you already trust). Ten pages come back that use only your columns. Pricing is fifteen US dollars per finished page, paid when you have the pages — see pricing.
