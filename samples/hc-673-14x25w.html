Sizing it right

  • Nominal is not the cut-out. 14 x 25 is the grille size, not the rough opening. Frame to the submittal dimension, or the grille will not seat and the flange will not cover the drywall edge.
  • Filter grille or plain return? This one carries the filter at the grille, so the filter is serviced from the room. If the filter lives at the air handler, you want the plain 673, not this.
  • White from the factory. Baked enamel over steel — the finish is applied before it ships, not sprayed on the frame in the field.
