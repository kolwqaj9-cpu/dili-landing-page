For people who received a clip or a private link

Check the book, then decide if we talk.

A short clip and a private link were sent from this shop. The lookup behind that link was built from a source that is already public — a maker's manual, a parts catalog, a product page. We are not the maker and we are not your company. Confirm the cites on the maker's own source before anyone works on a machine. A "host this on your site" mock is only a mock; it is not your shop.

What to do in two minutes

  1. Open the private link from the mail, if you still have it.
  2. Open the same document on the maker's own support site.
  3. See whether the checks on the page match the maker's own table and its shutdown or limit lines.
  4. If a cite is wrong, write us and say which one. If a technician would actually use a lookup like this, we can talk about the rest of the book.

What the mail should look like

The mail carries a real shop name, a real reply address at dilisights.com, and a plain text link. It does not carry a tracking shortener, and it does not pretend to be an internal ticket or a purchase order from inside your company. If a mail about us does not look like that, treat it as junk and tell us.

Do not want to hear from us again

Reply with one line — any line will do — or write to tony@dilisights.com with stop in the subject. We take the company off the list and confirm by mail, and we do not pass the address to anyone else.
