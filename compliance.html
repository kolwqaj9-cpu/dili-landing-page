For anyone who received a note, a clip or a private link

Here is who wrote to you.

The message came from Dilisights, the operating name of Strata Data LLC, a B2B digital marketing company in New Mexico, United States. We operate reference properties for industrial buyers and we connect those buyers with service providers. We are not the equipment maker whose manual appears in a clip, we are not your company, and we are not an employee or agent of either.

Why you got it

One of three reasons — the note will say which.

  1. You may be able to take the work. You are a third-party logistics provider, freight forwarder or electrical contractor, and a buyer using one of our properties described a job that fits what you do. We wrote to ask whether you want inquiries like that one.
  2. You asked us to find someone. You used one of our properties and asked for a provider. We are confirming what we sent you and where it went.
  3. A document of yours is on one of our pages. We built a reference page from a manual, catalog or public record that was already published. The page cites the source and the page number. If you want it taken down, say so and we will.

What the message should look like

A real company name, a real reply address at dilisights.com, and a plain link. It does not use a tracking shortener, and it does not pretend to be an internal ticket, a purchase order or a notice from inside your company. If a message about us does not look like that, treat it as junk and tell us — someone may be using our name.

If you do not want to hear from us again

Reply with one line — any line will do — or write to tony@dilisights.com with stop in the subject. We take you off the list and confirm by mail. We do not pass your details to anyone else, and we do not sell the same inquiry to two clients.

If you are a buyer and want out

Write the same address with remove in the subject and we delete your inquiry rather than passing it on. If it has already gone to a provider, we will tell you which one so you can deal with them directly. Privacy.
