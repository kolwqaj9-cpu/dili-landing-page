Privacy

Short version: this is a company page. We are not in the data business with your address, and the data we publish comes from public records.

This site

There is no mailing list to join, no advertising, no third-party analytics script and no tracking pixel on these pages. Our host keeps ordinary web server logs — address, time, page, user agent — for security and for working out what broke. We do not build profiles out of them and we do not sell them.

If you write to us

We keep the mail so we can answer it and so that a dispute about a page number has a record. We do not add you to a mailing list. If you ask us to stop writing to you, we confirm and we stop.

Documents

If you send us a document, it stays private. We do not put a client's manual, catalog, drawing or data extract on this public site, and we do not pass it to anyone else.

The data we publish

Reports come from files a public agency or registry has already published. We do not publish anything that identifies a private individual — no names of injured workers, patients or minors. Where a public file contains such a name, we strip it or we aggregate the row away.

Please ask us to stop

One line to tony@dilisights.com with stop in the subject is enough, or reply to any mail from us. Corrections, removals and questions: tony@dilisights.com. We answer.
