We hand you a buyer who already said what they need.

Three kinds of industrial service provider. One job for us in all three: find the buyer, check that the job is real and in your area, and deliver it to you and to nobody else.

Who we serve

Third-party logistics providers (3PL)

Shippers who need warehousing, cross-docking, last-mile or fulfilment and do not have it in house. The inquiry arrives with the lane, the volume, the handling profile and the start date the shipper gave us.

Freight forwarders

Importers and exporters who need a forwarder for a specific move — a lane, a mode, a commodity, a deadline. The inquiry arrives with the origin, the destination and the timing.

Commercial electrical contractors

Facility owners, property managers and general contractors who need electrical work scoped and quoted. The inquiry arrives with the property type, the work described in the buyer's own words, and the site location.

What you get

  • A described job, not a form dump. The buyer's own description of what they need, plus the fields that make it actionable for your scope.
  • Location and timing. Where the work is and when it has to happen, so you can rule it in or out in seconds.
  • One client per inquiry. We do not sell the same inquiry to three competitors. You and the buyer are the only parties to it.
  • A named source. Every inquiry says which of our properties it came from and when it arrived, so you can trace it.

What we do not do

We do not broker freight, we do not carry anything, and we are not a licensed contractor. We do not quote the job, sign for it, or take a percentage of your invoice. We do not promise a search ranking or a traffic number, and we do not guarantee that an inquiry converts — that part is your sales process, and we would be lying if we said otherwise.

A short call about your service area and the jobs you want, then a written scope with pricing, then a first batch. Write or call us. Pricing.
