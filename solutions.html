Two things: a cited lookup, and a published report.

Both are built out of documents that are already public. We add the reading work, not the invention.

1. Manual and catalog lookup pages

A page a shop technician can open, ask one question that is already in the book, and see the answer with the page number.

On the page

Title, the question, the steps as the book states them, and a cite back to the manual or catalog the maker already published. A code that is not in that book returns empty. We do not invent fault codes.

Price

Fifteen US dollars per finished page. Ten pages from the same public book are one hundred fifty dollars. Invoiced after you have the page. How to pay.

On your site or ours

The finished page can live on your own domain with your name on it. We hand over the folder. We are not your company and we do not sign as your employee.

2. Reports from public records

We take a public federal file, clean it, count it, and publish a page with inline charts, sortable tables and the CSV behind them.

What we read

Published agency files: occupational injury and inspection records, awards and contract announcements, corporate and nonprofit filings. Nothing private, nothing scraped from behind a login, nothing about an identifiable individual.

What you get

The published page names the file it came from and the day it was pulled, because agencies revise their data. Charts, tables and CSV may be republished free of charge with attribution. Custom cuts — by state, industry code, year or category — on request. The data desk.

What we will not do

We will not scrape your private systems. We will not send a "repair ticket" that pretends we work inside your company. We will not promise a search position. We will not put a number on a page without saying where the number came from.
