A small New Mexico company that publishes from public documents.

Dilisights is the operating name of Strata Data LLC, a limited liability company organized in New Mexico. Our work is taking documents anyone can already read — a maker's manual, a federal data file — and turning them into something a working person can actually use.

What we do

Cited lookups from published books. We take a manual or a parts catalog that a maker already publishes on its own site, pick a question that is genuinely answered inside that book, and build a one-question lookup page that shows the steps and the page numbers. What we make.

Reports from public records. We take a public federal file, clean it, count it, and publish a page with charts, tables and a CSV download, on the site of the industry the file belongs to. The data desk.
