A small New Mexico company that finds customers for industrial service providers.

Dilisights is the operating name of Strata Data LLC, a limited liability company organized in New Mexico. We are a B2B digital marketing company. We build the properties that industrial buyers read, and we are paid by the service providers who win the work that comes out of them.

What we do

We find the demand. Industrial buyers — a plant maintenance lead, a shop owner, a dispatcher — search for an answer before they look for a vendor. We build reference properties that answer that question: page-cited lookups from a maker's own published manual or parts catalog, and reports built from public federal records. Where the leads come from.

We qualify it, then we hand it over. An inquiry from one of our properties arrives with the job described in the buyer's own words, the location, and the timing. We pass the ones that fit a client's service area and scope to that client, and only to that client. How it works.

We are paid for the inquiries we deliver. Pricing is agreed in writing before any inquiry is sent, and we do not take a percentage of the client's invoice. Pricing.

Who we serve

Third-party logistics (3PL) providers, freight forwarders, and commercial electrical contractors serving industrial and commercial clients in the United States. What we do for them.
